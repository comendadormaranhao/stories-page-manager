Stories Page Manager

Stories Page Manager · operated by SC ONLINE VENTURE LTDA · last updated October 16, 2026

Stories Page Manager is an internal tool operated by SC ONLINE VENTURE LTDA to manage the Facebook Pages that we own or administer. It publishes our own videos on those Pages, posts the Page's subscription invitation as a comment, and replies to comments that people leave on our posts.

The tool is used only by our team, and only on Pages where an authorized administrator has granted access.
