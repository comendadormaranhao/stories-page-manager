Privacy Policy

Stories Page Manager · operated by SC ONLINE VENTURE LTDA · last updated October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Stories Page Manager (the "App"), operated by SC ONLINE VENTURE LTDA ("we"), receives from Meta Platforms and how we use it.

Who uses the App

The App is an internal tool. It is used only by our team to manage Facebook Pages that we own or administer. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • Page information and Page access tokens granted by a Page administrator (Page ID, name, username).
  • Posts and videos published by our Pages, and their engagement counts.
  • Comments that people publicly post on our Pages' posts: comment text, comment ID, time, and, when Meta provides it, the commenter's public name.

How we use it

  • To publish our own videos on our Pages and keep a schedule.
  • To post a comment from the Page inviting viewers to subscribe to the Page.
  • To write and post a reply from the Page to comments on our posts, and to skip spam or offensive comments.

We do not sell, rent or share this information for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles of individuals.

Service providers

To draft replies, the text of a comment and a short summary of our own video are sent to a text-generation service (currently Groq, Inc.). No access tokens, account data or contact information are sent. The service returns a draft reply, which the App checks against our rules before posting.

Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored only on our own computers, outside any public location. We keep a log of the comments we replied to (comment ID, text and our reply) for up to 12 months to avoid duplicate replies, then delete it.

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete your own comment on Facebook at any time. To ask us to delete any record that mentions your comment, follow the steps on the Data Deletion page or email lucasgameninja@gmail.com.
