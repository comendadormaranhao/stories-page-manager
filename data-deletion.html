Data Deletion Instructions

Stories Page Manager · operated by SC ONLINE VENTURE LTDA · last updated October 16, 2026

Stories Page Manager keeps only a small log of comments it replied to on our Facebook Pages (comment ID, comment text and our reply). It does not keep any account data.

How to delete your data

  1. To remove your comment from Facebook, delete it directly on the post. Our record of it is removed on our next cleanup.
  2. To ask us to delete any record that mentions your comment right away, email lucasgameninja@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request and the link to your comment or the Page and date.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the matching records within 30 days.

Page administrators

To remove the App's access to a Page, go to Facebook Settings > Business Integrations (or Business Settings > Apps) and remove Stories Page Manager. Its Page access tokens stop working immediately; we then delete them from our systems.
